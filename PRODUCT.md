# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Regular indoor boulderers logging climbs between attempts. They are often standing in a dim gym with chalky hands, holding the phone in one hand, and unwilling to perform administrative work. Their primary job is to preserve a useful memory of a climb before returning to the wall.

## Product Purpose

Crux turns a route photo into a durable problem card, generates a route mask automatically, and records a deliberate outcome with as little interruption as possible. The core loop is photo, route preview, outcome, saved. Success means a climber can log a problem in under 15 seconds, trust that it is stored, and keep climbing.

## Brand Personality

Focused, athletic, rewarding.

The interface should combine Linear's product discipline with Nike Run Club's athletic warmth. It should feel sleek and calm during capture, then briefly expressive when a climber records a send or flash.

## Anti-references

- Technical terminals, lab consoles, and machine-oriented labels such as `ADD_DATA`, `TERMINATE`, or internal identifiers.
- Analytics dashboards that put metrics ahead of the next climbing action.
- Generic gamified fitness feeds with streak pressure, noisy badges, or manufactured competition.
- Wellness-product softness that makes the interface feel detached from a physical climbing gym.
- Decorative cards, gradients, glass effects, or motion that compete with the route photo.

## Design Principles

1. **The next climb is always closest.** Reuse or create the active session automatically and keep capture within one thumb reach.
2. **The photo is the interface.** Let route imagery and the generated mask carry the hierarchy instead of surrounding them with controls.
3. **Ask only what is required now.** Require a deliberate outcome; reveal attempts, grade, notes, sharing, and mask correction only when requested or needed.
4. **Reward the meaningful moment.** Recording a send or flash earns concise motion, haptic feedback, and confident confirmation without delaying the next capture.
5. **Make reliability visible but quiet.** Local save and sync status should be understandable without becoming primary actions.
6. **Prefer climbing language.** Use direct human labels such as Log a climb, Sent, Tried, Fix route, and Finish session.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA on the web and equivalent accessible semantics on mobile.
- Maintain touch targets of at least 44 points and optimize primary actions for one-handed reach.
- Support reduced motion and never depend on animation to communicate saved state.
- Do not rely on color alone for outcomes, confidence, errors, or sync status.
- Provide strong contrast for dim gym lighting while avoiding pure black and pure white.
- Avoid interactions that require precise fine-motor control except inside the optional mask editor.
